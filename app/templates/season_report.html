Project background and my role

The project began with recording statistics for Jordan Christian Preparatory basketball games. Organizing those records in a spreadsheet made it possible to examine team and player performance across the season.

My work involved recording game statistics, structuring player-game observations, checking calculations, analyzing performance, and creating comparisons and visualizations. The analytical work came before the software project.

Repeated calculations and the limitations of the spreadsheet workflow motivated CourtStats: a reusable application shaped by the practical experience of collecting and analyzing basketball data.

Dataset overview

The dataset covers 15 games and represents 8 players in 120 player-game observations. The team record in this sample is 7 wins and 8 losses.

Derived metrics

Field goals, shooting percentages, total rebounds, and assist-to-turnover ratios are calculated from raw statistics. Season totals, averages, and comparisons require validated records and clearly defined denominators.

A player-game record does not establish that a player participated. The original spreadsheet does not formally distinguish PLAYED from DNP (did not play).

Limitations

Responsible interpretation begins with what the dataset cannot tell us.

Sample and context
Only 15 games from one team. The data is observational, so associations do not establish causation. Opponent quality and schedule differences can affect comparisons.
Arithmetic and event verification
Internal validation confirms arithmetic consistency. Individual recorded events cannot be independently verified without the original paper or video source.
Participation and opportunity
No minutes played and no explicit PLAYED/DNP status in the original spreadsheet. Per-minute, per-36, and usage-rate analysis are not supported.
Game detail
No quarter-by-quarter scoring or shot locations. Quarter splits, clutch performance, and shot-location analysis cannot be reconstructed reliably.
Opponent statistics and possessions
No opponent box-score dataset for shooting, rebounds, or turnovers. Opponent percentages, rebounding margin, exact pace, and possession-based offensive or defensive ratings are not supported.
Lineups and impact
No lineup combinations, plus/minus, or on/off data. Individual impact and lineup chemistry cannot be inferred from these records.

What I would collect and build next

Future work starts with better evidence. New analyses would require additional collection and validation before they could support conclusions.

Minutes and DNP status
Distinguish participation from absence and describe production relative to playing opportunity.
Quarter splits and shot locations
Add temporal and spatial context to scoring and shooting analysis.
Opponent box scores and game context
Support stronger possession estimates and, with sufficient schedule context, opponent-adjusted comparisons.
Lineup and substitution data
Create a foundation for lineup analysis with clear definitions of who was on the court and when.
